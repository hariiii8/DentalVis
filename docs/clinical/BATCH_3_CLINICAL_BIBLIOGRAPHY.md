## 2. Specific Audit Findings & Applied Corrections

1. **Gingival Flap Surgery (`gingival-flap-surgery`):**
   - *Previous Error:* Associated with DOI `10.1111/jcpe.13329` (a study on probiotic lozenges).
   - *Corrected Reference:* EFP S3 Level Clinical Practice Guideline (Sanz M et al., 2020), DOI: [10.1111/jcpe.13290](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpe.13290). Fully supports Step 3 flap surgical intervention for persistent $\ge 6\text{ mm}$ pockets.
2. **Alveolar Ridge Augmentation (`alveolar-ridge-augmentation`):**
   - *Previous Error:* Associated with DOI `10.1111/jcpe.13110` (a 20-year follow-up study on GTR in intrabony defects).
   - *Corrected Reference:* 15th European Workshop on Periodontology Group 4 Consensus Report (Jepsen S et al., 2019), DOI: [10.1111/jcpe.13121](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpe.13121). Directly supports horizontal and vertical GBR with particulate biomaterials and barrier membranes.
3. **Crown Lengthening (`crown-lengthening`):**
   - *Previous Error:* Associated with DOI `10.1902/jop.2015.150478`.
   - *Corrected Reference:* Systematic review on pre-restorative crown lengthening outcomes by Pilalas I et al. (2016), *J Clin Periodontol*, DOI: [10.1111/jcpe.12617](https://doi.org/10.1111/jcpe.12617). Supports biologic width, supracrestal attachment, and ferrule benchmarks.
4. **Sinus Lift (`sinus-lift`):**
   - *Previous Error:* Associated with unindexed string DOI `10.11607/jomi.18suppl.g3`.
   - *Corrected Reference:* Landmark systematic review on lateral sinus floor elevation by Pjetursson BE, Tan WC, Zwahlen M, Lang NP (2008), *J Clin Periodontol*, DOI: [10.1111/j.1600-051X.2008.01272.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-051X.2008.01272.x).
5. **Dental Abscess Management (`dental-abscess-management`):**
   - *Scope Delimitation:* Clearly separated the 2019 ADA Clinical Practice Guideline (Lockhart PB et al., DOI: [10.1016/j.adaj.2019.08.020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adaj.2019.08.020)) on conservative antibiotic stewardship in localized odontogenic swellings from emergency deep fascial space infection protocols. Added Flynn TR et al. (2006, *JOMS*, DOI: [10.1016/j.joms.2006.03.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joms.2006.03.015)) for Ludwig's angina and deep-space surgical management.
6. **Implant-Supported Bridge (`implant-supported-bridge`):**
   - *Previous Error:* Associated with journal front matter DOI `10.1111/clr.13320`.
   - *Corrected Reference:* Landmark systematic review on implant-supported FDPs after $\ge 5$ years by Pjetursson BE et al. (2012), *Clin Oral Implants Res*, DOI: [10.1111/j.1600-0501.2012.02546.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0501.2012.02546.x).
7. **Full-Arch Implant Restoration (`full-arch-implant-restoration`):**
   - *Previous Error:* Associated with unverified string DOI `10.11607/jomi.18suppl.g2`.
   - *Corrected Reference:* Group 2 ITI Consensus Report on prosthodontics and full-arch implant restorations by Morton D, Gallucci G, Lin WS, Pjetursson B, Polido W, et al. (2018), *Clin Oral Implants Res*, DOI: [10.1111/clr.13298](https://doi.org/10.1111/clr.13298).
8. **Socket Preservation (`socket-preservation-bone-graft`):**
   - *Evidence Nuance:* Reflected Cochrane review limitations (Atieh MA et al., 2015/2021, DOI: [10.1002/14651858.CD010176.pub2](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD010176.pub2)); removed any promise of totally preventing bone resorption or guaranteed future implant survival.
